# Appendix B. Search strings, counts and other sources (version 3, 31 October 2026)

All sources use the same word blocks; only the syntax changes. Code and logs: `00_protocol/search_development/` (`search_blocks.py`, `search_dev.py`, `search_other_dbs.py`, `search_dev_log.csv`, `benchmark_recall*.csv`). No date or language limit is applied at the interface.

## B1. Word blocks

- **FEATURE:** lighting OR illumination OR "street lights" OR "street lighting" OR "street lamps" OR vegetation OR greenery OR "green space" OR "green spaces" OR landscaping OR sightlines OR "sight lines" OR "prospect-refuge" OR "prospect and refuge" OR entrapment OR concealment OR "active frontage" OR "active frontages" OR "shop fronts" OR "mixed use" OR "mixed-use" OR incivilities OR "physical disorder" OR "neighborhood disorder" OR "neighbourhood disorder" OR "social disorder" OR graffiti OR litter OR "broken windows" OR "presence of others" OR "presence of people" OR "natural surveillance" OR "eyes on the street" OR "built environment" OR "urban design" OR "environmental design" OR CPTED OR "physical environment"
- **PERCEIVED:** "perceived safety" OR "fear of crime" OR "feeling of safety" OR "feelings of safety" OR "sense of safety" OR "perception of safety" OR "perceptions of safety" OR "perceived security" OR "feeling safe" OR "feel safe" OR "feelings of insecurity" OR reassurance OR "perceived danger" OR "perceived risk of crime" OR "fear of victimization" OR "fear of victimisation" OR "safety perception" OR "safety perceptions" OR "feel unsafe" OR "feeling unsafe" OR "felt unsafe" OR "perceived personal safety"
- **ACTUAL:** "sexual harassment" OR "street harassment" OR "sexual assault" OR "sexual violence" OR "violence against women" OR "gender-based violence" OR "crime against women" OR "crimes against women" OR victimization OR victimisation OR rape
- **WOMEN:** women OR woman OR female OR females OR gender OR girls OR "sex differences"
- **PLACE:** environment OR environments OR "public space" OR "public spaces" OR park OR parks OR street OR streets OR neighbourhood OR neighborhood OR urban OR city OR transport OR transit
- **FRENCH (S3):** "sentiment de sécurité" OR "sentiment d'insécurité" OR "peur du crime" OR "harcèlement de rue"

Strategies: **S1** = FEATURE AND PERCEIVED; **S2** = FEATURE AND ACTUAL AND WOMEN; **S3** = FRENCH (French-language outcome terms); **S4** = PERCEIVED AND WOMEN AND PLACE. The final set is the union of S1, S2, S3 and S4, deduplicated.

## B2b. PRESS 2015 self-check (McGowan et al., 2016)

There was no peer review of the search by another person. I checked the six PRESS elements myself:

| PRESS element | Check |
|---|---|
| Translation of the research question | The word blocks map to exposure (features), outcomes (perceived and actual safety), population (women/gender) and setting (place); S4 covers gender-focused studies without a feature word |
| Boolean and proximity operators | OR within blocks, AND between blocks; no NOT; proximity operators not used because OpenAlex and Semantic Scholar do not support them |
| Subject headings | Not used: OpenAlex, Semantic Scholar and CORE have no controlled vocabulary; in PubMed, MeSH terms for this topic are too broad, so title and abstract words are used in all sources for consistency |
| Text word searching | Spelling variants (victimization/victimisation, neighborhood/neighbourhood), phrase forms, French terms (S3); single generic words removed after version 1 because they pulled in unrelated topics |
| Spelling, syntax and line numbers | Each string is generated by code from one list of blocks (`search_blocks.py`), so no hand-typing errors between sources; every string that was run during search development in OpenAlex, Semantic Scholar and PubMed ran without syntax error and was logged; the problems found with CORE are described in B3 |
| Limits and filters | None at the interface (no date, language or document-type limit); language is applied at screening |
