# PCI RR Stage 1: what the protocol must satisfy

Source: PCI Registered Reports, Guide for Authors (rr.peercommunityin.org/help/guide_for_authors), read on 27 October 2026. Quotes are short; section numbers refer to that guide.

## Review criteria for Stage 1 (§2.1)
| Criterion | What reviewers check | Where our protocol answers it |
|---|---|---|
| 1A | The research question is scientifically valid (novelty and importance are *not* judged) | Introduction, aims |
| 1B | Hypotheses are logical, precise, stated in terms of variables (optional if the study is estimation-only) | Hypotheses + design table |
| 1C | Methods and analyses are sound and feasible, including a sampling plan / sensitivity justification | Methods, sampling plan, power/sensitivity |
| 1D | Enough detail to replicate exactly and to prevent undisclosed flexibility; a **study design table** is strongly advised | Methods + design table |
| 1E | Outcome-neutral checks that show the data can answer the question (positive controls, quality checks) | Outcome-neutral checks section |

## Practical rules
- **Study design table** (§2.16): mandatory for full Stage 1 manuscripts that test hypotheses. Columns: Question · Hypothesis · Sampling plan · Analysis plan · Rationale for deciding the sensitivity of the test · Interpretation given different outcomes · Theory that could be shown wrong.
- **Existing data** (§2.6): meta-analyses reach Level 1 to 3; the authors declare their level and certify what they have and have not seen. Extra countermeasures at Levels 1–2 are generally waived for meta-analyses. Studies whose outcomes are already known to the authors cannot be submitted.
- **Format** (§2.22): no word limit and no imposed format; as long as needed for reproducibility.
- **Deposit** (§2.17): the Stage 1 manuscript must be deposited in a recognised repository (OSF, Zenodo), either public or embargoed with a view-only link.
- **Tracks** (§2.20): standard (first review round about 4–8 weeks) or scheduled (one-page snapshot first, review fixed at least 6 weeks ahead).
- **Closures**: no submissions from 1 December to mid-January and from early July to late August.
- **Independent researchers** (§2.1): may receive enhanced initial screening.
- **AI use** (§2.28): describe how AI was used in the Methods or Acknowledgements; never use AI to generate illustrations; non-compliance leads to rejection.
- **Data and code** (§2.11): all data, materials and code public at Stage 2.
- **Stage 2** (§2.8, §2.10): Introduction and Methods stay as approved (tense changes only); the abstract gives the Stage 1 URL and the date of in-principle acceptance; deviations are footnoted.
